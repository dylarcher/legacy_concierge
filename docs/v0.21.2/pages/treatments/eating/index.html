Eating Disorders & Monitoring

Navigating an eating disorder can feel isolating, but Legacy Concierge offers world-class care to guide you toward healing with compassion. Our elite concierge nurses, selected for their unwavering dedication, provide nuanced support that nurtures your well-being and fosters longevity with expert sensitivity.

Medical Monitoring
Our concierge nurses regularly assess vital signs (e.g., heart rate, blood pressure, weight) and monitor for physical complications of eating disorders, such as electrolyte imbalances, dehydration, or cardiac irregularities, promptly reporting findings to physicians.
Medication Management
For clients prescribed medications to manage co-occurring conditions (e.g., antidepressants, anti-anxiety drugs), our nurses ensure proper adherence, monitor side effects, and coordinate with prescribing providers to support your treatment.
Meal Support
Our nurses provide supervision or encouragement during meals to support adherence to meal plans, reduce anxiety around eating, and discourage disordered behaviors like restriction or purging, working closely with dietitians or therapists.
Care Coordination
Our nurses act as liaisons between you, therapists, dietitians, psychiatrists, and other providers, ensuring seamless collaboration to optimize your treatment plan and recovery outcomes.
Emotional Support
Through empathetic engagement, our nurses offer emotional support to help you navigate the stress, shame, or anxiety associated with eating disorders, fostering a trusting relationship tailored to your needs.
Relapse Prevention
Our nurses observe for signs of relapse, such as changes in eating habits, weight fluctuations, or compensatory behaviors, and collaborate with your treatment team to intervene early and support sustained recovery.
Patient Education
We educate you and your chosen family or community about eating disorders, treatment strategies, and healthy coping mechanisms, while guiding families on providing supportive, non-judgmental care.
Wellness Promotion
Our nurses promote overall well-being by encouraging balanced routines, stress management techniques (e.g., mindfulness, journaling), and healthy sleep habits, all tailored to support your recovery journey.
Emergency Response
In cases of acute distress or medical emergencies (e.g., severe malnutrition or electrolyte imbalances), our nurses provide immediate response and coordinate with medical professionals or emergency services to ensure your safety.
Appointment Support
Our nurses may accompany you to therapy sessions, medical check-ups, or nutrition consultations, offering emotional support and ensuring treatment compliance, tailored to your preferences.
