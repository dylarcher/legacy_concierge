Cardiac & Pulmonary Oversight

Managing cardiopulmonary conditions requires more than medical oversight—it demands compassionate, world-class support tailored to your life. Our elite concierge nurses, handpicked for their unwavering expertise, deliver nuanced cardiopulmonary care that strengthens your well-being and nurtures your longevity with precision.

Personalized Care, Personalized Solutions

With Legacy Concierge, your health journey becomes a seamless blend of comfort, confidence, and renewed strength.

Vital Signs Monitoring

Our nurses regularly assess vital signs (e.g., heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate) to monitor cardiopulmonary function, detecting early signs of deterioration, such as arrhythmias, heart failure exacerbation, or respiratory distress.

Medication Management

Our nurses ensure adherence to prescribed medications (e.g., beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors, bronchodilators, or anticoagulants), monitor for side effects, and coordinate with physicians for dosage adjustments or new prescriptions, supporting your treatment with care.

Symptom Management

Our nurses help manage symptoms like chest pain, shortness of breath, fatigue, or edema, implementing tailored care plans to alleviate discomfort and coordinating with specialists for advanced interventions.

Post-Procedure Care

After cardiopulmonary procedures (e.g., stent placement, bypass surgery, or lung surgery), our nurses provide in-home care, including wound care, monitoring for infections, and supporting recovery to prevent readmissions.

Care Coordination

Our nurses act as liaisons between you, cardiologists, pulmonologists, and other providers, ensuring seamless collaboration to optimize your treatment plan and health outcomes.

Lifestyle Guidance

Our nurses guide you on heart- and lung-healthy habits, such as low-sodium diets, smoking cessation, exercise tailored to your condition, stress reduction techniques, and proper use of inhalers or oxygen therapy, promoting overall well-being.

Patient Education

We educate you and your chosen family or community about cardiopulmonary conditions (e.g., COPD, heart failure, asthma), treatment options, and self-management strategies, empowering them to recognize warning signs and take proactive steps.

Emergency Preparedness

Our nurses train you and your family to recognize emergencies (e.g., heart attack, severe asthma attack) and provide immediate response during acute episodes, coordinating with emergency services when needed.

Pulmonary Rehabilitation

For chronic lung conditions, our nurses assist with breathing exercises, energy conservation techniques, and adherence to pulmonary rehab plans, tailored to improve respiratory function and stamina.

Appointment Support

Our nurses may accompany you to medical visits, cardiac rehab sessions, or pulmonary function tests, offering emotional support and ensuring compliance with care plans, tailored to your preferences.
