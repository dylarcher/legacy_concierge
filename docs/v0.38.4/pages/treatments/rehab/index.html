Rehab & Addiction Recovery

The journey to sobriety is deeply personal, and Legacy Concierge is here to support you with world-class care that honors your strength. Our elite concierge nurses, chosen for their unwavering commitment, deliver bespoke recovery support that nurtures your well-being and fosters longevity with expert care and discreet sensitivity.

Transition Support
After inpatient or outpatient rehab, concierge nurses help clients transition back to daily life by monitoring their recovery, reinforcing coping strategies, and ensuring adherence to aftercare plans.
Medication Management
For clients prescribed medications to manage withdrawal symptoms, cravings, or co-occurring mental health conditions (e.g., methadone, buprenorphine, or antidepressants), nurses oversee proper use, monitor side effects, and coordinate with prescribing physicians.
Sobriety Support
Concierge nurses provide regular check-ins to support sobriety, observe for signs of relapse, and offer encouragement. They may also administer or coordinate drug and alcohol testing if part of the recovery plan.
Care Coordination
They act as a bridge between clients, addiction specialists, therapists, and rehab facilities, ensuring seamless communication and follow-through on treatment recommendations.
Emotional Support
Through empathetic listening and trust-based relationships, nurses offer emotional support to help clients navigate the challenges of recovery, such as cravings, stress, or feelings of isolation.
Relapse Prevention
Concierge nurses are trained to recognize early warning signs of relapse and can intervene by providing immediate support, using de-escalation techniques, or contacting addiction professionals or emergency services if needed.
Lifestyle Coaching
They promote recovery through guidance on healthy habits, including nutrition, exercise, sleep hygiene, and stress management techniques like mindfulness or meditation, which support long-term sobriety.
Family Education
Concierge nurses educate families about addiction and recovery, helping them understand how to support their loved one while setting healthy boundaries.
Appointment Support
They may accompany clients to therapy sessions, support group meetings (e.g., AA or NA), or medical appointments, ensuring consistency in treatment and providing moral support.
Mental Health Integration
Many individuals in recovery have co-occurring mental health issues (e.g., anxiety, depression). Concierge nurses monitor these conditions, coordinate care, and integrate mental health support.
