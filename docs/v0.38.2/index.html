CONCIERGE NURSING VS. HOME CARE

Both services embody Legacy's commitment to compassion, dignity, and excellence - but each is designed for a distinct level of need.

Service Type Concierge Nursing Home Care
Who Provides Care Licensed Registered Nurses (RNs) Certified Home Health Aides (HHAs)
Scope of Care Medical: clinical assessments, wound care, IV therapy, medication management, symptom monitoring Non-medical: companionship, mobility support, daily activities, meal prep
Care Plan Customized medical oversight with physician coordination Personalized lifestyle support plan
When It's Best Used Post-surgery recovery, chronic illness, complex health management Daily assistance, independent living support, elder companionship

Both are delivered with the same promise: Legacy-level care, trust, and respect.

OUR PROCESS

Designed for Clinical Precision and Complete Peace of Mind

At Legacy Concierge, every client's journey begins with an uncompromising standard of medical excellence. Our four-step process ensures your care is clinically precise, fully coordinated, and personally tailored — from your first call to your final recovery milestone.

Comprehensive Medical &
Environmental Assessment

Comprehensive Medical &

Environmental Assessment

Our clinical team begins with a 360° evaluation of your health status, lifestyle, and environment. We review your medical history, current conditions, surgical plans, and physician notes, then conduct an in-home or virtual assessment to identify everything needed for safe, optimized recovery — including mobility requirements, nutrition, and emotional wellbeing.

Build Your
Personalized Care Team

Build Your

Personalized Care Team

We hand-select your dedicated care team, led by a concierge registered nurse (RN) and supported by specialists across our elite network — including physicians, physical therapists, nutritionists, and wellness experts.

Every professional assigned is vetted for clinical expertise, emotional intelligence, and alignment with your personal goals. Whether you require 24-hour RN presence, shift-based oversight, or multidisciplinary coordination, we create a structure that ensures consistency, safety, and discretion.

Develop & Approve
Your Personalized Care Plan

Develop & Approve

Your Personalized Care Plan

Your plan of care is not templated — it's engineered. We integrate input from your surgeons, primary care physicians, and specialists, synthesizing complex medical directives into a clear, actionable plan tailored to your condition and environment.

This includes:

  • Medication and wound management protocols
  • IV or infusion therapy scheduling
  • Rehabilitation and mobility planning
  • Nutritional and recovery timelines
  • Environmental safety adaptations
  • Real-time communication with your care team and family

Every plan is reviewed by our clinical leadership for accuracy, oversight, and compliance with state and federal healthcare standards.

Begin Ongoing Clinical
Oversight & Adaptive Care

Begin Ongoing Clinical

Oversight & Adaptive Care

Once care begins, your concierge nurse manages daily medical tasks, continuous vitals tracking, and proactive symptom monitoring. Behind the scenes, our clinical directors review reports, ensuring early detection of complications and immediate intervention when necessary.

We maintain 24/7 care coordination, seamless physician communication, and real-time updates through secure digital platforms. If your needs evolve, your plan adapts — instantly.
