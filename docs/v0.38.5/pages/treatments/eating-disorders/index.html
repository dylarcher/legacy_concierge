Eating Disorders & Monitoring

Navigating an eating disorder can feel isolating, but Legacy Concierge offers world-class care to guide you toward healing with compassion. Our elite concierge nurses, handpicked for their unwavering dedication to patient well-being, deliver personalized support that nurtures your recovery with precision and care.

Health Monitoring
Our nurses regularly assess vital signs and physical health markers to monitor for complications associated with eating disorders, detecting early signs of medical concerns such as electrolyte imbalances, cardiac irregularities, or nutritional deficiencies.
Medication Management
Our nurses ensure adherence to prescribed medications, monitor for side effects, and coordinate with physicians and psychiatrists for dosage adjustments or new prescriptions, supporting your treatment plan with attentive care.
Meal Support & Monitoring
Our nurses provide compassionate supervision during meals, offering behavioral support and encouragement while monitoring food intake and helping establish healthy eating patterns in a non-judgmental environment.
Treatment Team Coordination
Our nurses act as liaisons between you, therapists, dietitians, psychiatrists, and other providers, ensuring seamless collaboration to optimize your treatment plan and recovery outcomes.
Emotional & Psychological Support
Our nurses offer empathetic engagement and relationship building, providing a compassionate presence that supports your emotional well-being throughout the recovery process.
Relapse Prevention
Our nurses monitor for behavioral warning signs and provide early intervention strategies, helping you navigate triggers and maintain progress on your path to recovery.
Education & Family Support
We educate you and your family about eating disorders, recovery strategies, and self-management techniques, empowering everyone involved to recognize warning signs and provide supportive care.
Lifestyle & Wellness Coaching
Our nurses guide you on stress management, healthy routines, and wellness practices that support recovery, promoting overall well-being and sustainable lifestyle changes.
Crisis Intervention
Our nurses provide immediate response during medical or psychological emergencies, coordinating with emergency services when needed and ensuring your safety at all times.
Appointment Accompaniment
Our nurses may accompany you to therapy sessions, medical visits, or nutritional consultations, offering emotional support and ensuring compliance with your care plan.
